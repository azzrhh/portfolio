Utopia / Dystopia

A branching choice-based narrative game exploring the future of human designers in the age of AI. Players sit at a virtual desk and make four small decisions — each one pushing their career toward creative fulfillment or quiet obsolescence.

Role Designer & Developer
Tools HTML, CSS, JS, Typed.js
Year 2025
Published GitHub Pages

The Concept

The Question

As AI tools grow capable of generating layouts, branding, graphics, and websites, what happens to the value and identity of human designers? This project doesn't answer that question — it makes you live it.

Players are placed at a designer's desk and asked to make four everyday professional decisions. Each choice between the human path and the AI-assisted path reveals a short glimpse of the future that decision leads to. Together, the four choices paint a portrait of the designer you're becoming.

Three Design Lenses

Speculative Design
The game imagines near-future scenarios grounded in today's real pressures — the pull of efficiency, the seduction of automation, the fear of falling behind.
Challenging Mental Models
It questions the assumption that design work must be fast and data-driven, asking whether creative authenticity and human instinct still have a place.
Utopian / Dystopian Narrative
Neither path is villainized. The dystopia is comfortable and profitable. The utopia is slower and uncertain. The tension lives in that ambiguity.

The experience unfolds in four stages, designed to transition the player gradually from observer to active participant.

The 4 Scenarios

Each desk object presents a real moment in a designer's working life — and a fork in the road between intentional human practice and convenient automation.

Notebook

"You have some rough ideas for a new client project, but you're short on time. You open your notebook to start organizing your thoughts..."

Utopia

Sketch it all out by hand — slower, but your thoughts feel more personal and focused.

"You build a habit of reflective, personal design exploration."

Dystopia

Let the AI summarize and organize your notes into wireframes — faster and more efficient.

"You begin to lose touch with your creative instincts. Your work feels fast, but generic."

Calendar

"You've been meaning to build your skills, but you can't decide how to spend your time next month. Your calendar's open. A reminder pings..."

Utopia

Plan yourself and write it down by hand, guided by what matters to you.

"You are clear about what comes next. You are ready to take the next step."

Dystopia

Let AI schedule and check for availabilities.

"You don't have to spend time thinking about what to plan. AI will do it for you."

Phone

"You just got a message from a new client. It's vague, rushed, and includes an AI-generated brief. You're tempted to just say yes and move on, but..."

Utopia

Ask questions, clarify values, and shape the brief into something more meaningful.

"The client is impressed by your insight. The project feels collaborative and intentional."

Dystopia

Let AI auto-respond and auto-generate the first round of designs.

"Clients stop treating you like a creative partner. You're a service, not a voice."

Post-it

"You sit down to write some career goals. Your AI assistant suggests starting with templates based on market demand. You hesitate — do you let it help?"

Utopia

Write your goals from scratch, guided by your values and curiosities.

"Your goals evolve, rooted in your curiosity. They lead you down unexpected but fulfilling paths."

Dystopia

Use AI to generate optimized goals based on salary trends and hiring stats.

"Your career follows a smooth, profitable curve. But each milestone feels like someone else's win."

Technical Implementation

Built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks. Six key decisions shaped the implementation.

Typed.js Integration

Each scenario uses Typed.js to render the setup text as a typewriter animation at 10 chars/sec, drawing players into the narrative before choices appear.

Timed State Machine

The flow transitions automatically: START triggers an 8-second intro, then the desk loads. Each item click types for 5 seconds before the binary options reveal themselves.

Event-Driven Interactions

Click handlers on each desk object trigger the scenario. Close button and ESC key both call closeAllContent(), which resets all state and restores the desk view.

Single Typed Instance

A shared typedInstance variable is destroyed and recreated on each new interaction, preventing animation conflicts when the player switches between desk items.

SVG Desk Assets

All six desk objects — notebook, calendar, phone, post-it, cup, clock — are SVG files, precisely positioned via CSS. Two (cup, clock) are decorative only; four are interactive.

Hand-Drawn Illustrations

All eight outcome images (four utopia, four dystopia) are original hand-drawn sketches. The raw, imperfect line quality was a deliberate contrast to AI's polished outputs.
